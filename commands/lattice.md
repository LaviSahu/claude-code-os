Run a full Mental Models Lens (per the latticework section in my global CLAUDE.md) on this decision:

**$ARGUMENTS**

1. **Frame it** — restate the decision in one sentence, note what makes it big or hard to reverse, and what "good" looks like.
2. **Pick 4–7 relevant models across disciplines** — always consider inversion, second-order effects, opportunity cost, base rates, incentives, margin of safety, circle of competence, and misjudgment biases; include at least one non-obvious model and say why it applies.
3. **Walk each model separately, named** — a short paragraph each: what this lens says about this decision, concretely.
4. **Surface disagreements** — where the models point in different directions is where the clarity lives; spell out the tension rather than smoothing it over.
5. **Synthesize** — a clear recommendation with confidence level, what evidence would change it, and at most 2 concrete next actions.

Be honest first — if the lenses collectively say "don't do this", say so plainly.
